How Delivery Works

From Our Shop To Your Door

A simple, transparent process. You'll always know where your package is.

1 Place Your Order

Chat with us on WhatsApp or visit our shop. Confirm your device, price, and delivery address.

2 Confirm & Pay

Pay via bank transfer, cash on delivery (Aba only), or POS. You'll receive a receipt immediately.
