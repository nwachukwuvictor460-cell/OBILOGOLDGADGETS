Full Policy

The Details

Everything you need to know about how we handle your information at OBILO GOLD GADGETS.

1 Information We Collect

When you message us, place an order, or subscribe to our broadcast, we may collect: your name, WhatsApp number, email (if provided), delivery address, and order preferences. We do not collect payment card details — all payments are processed securely via bank transfer, POS, or cash.

2 How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively to: process and deliver your order, provide warranty support, respond to your inquiries, and (if you opt in) send you updates about new arrivals and deals. We never use your data for any other purpose.

3 Third-Party Sharing

We only share the minimum required information with our trusted courier partners (name, phone number, and delivery address) so they can deliver your package. No other third party has access to your data.

4 Data Retention

We keep your order history for up to 24 months for warranty and support purposes. After that, it's automatically deleted. Chat logs are retained for 12 months unless you request earlier deletion.

5 Your Consent

By using our website or contacting us, you consent to this privacy policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp and asking to be removed from our records.

6 Updates to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with the updated date. Significant changes will also be announced on our WhatsApp broadcast list.
