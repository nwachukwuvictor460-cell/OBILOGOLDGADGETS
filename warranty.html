✦ Our Promise

1-Year Warranty
On Every Gadget

Every device you buy from OBILO GOLD GADGETS comes with a full 12-month warranty. If something goes wrong, we make it right — no stress, no stories.

What We Cover

Full Coverage, Real Support

Our warranty covers genuine manufacturing defects and hardware faults. Whether it's a battery issue, screen fault, or internal component failure — we've got you protected.

◈

Manufacturing Defects

Any fault that comes from the factory or is caused by faulty components is fully covered for 12 months.

⚡

Battery & Charging

Battery health drops or charging port failures within the warranty period are repaired or replaced free.

◉

Screen & Display

Dead pixels, touch failure, or display malfunctions caused by hardware (not accidental damage) are covered.

⌘

Internal Hardware

Motherboard, camera, speaker, and other internal component failures are handled under warranty.

◐

Software Faults

System crashes or software bugs that can't be fixed with a simple reset are repaired by our technicians.

✦

Free Diagnostics

Not sure what's wrong? Bring it in — we'll diagnose the issue for free within the warranty period.

How It Works

Claiming Your Warranty

A simple 4-step process. We handle everything from diagnostics to repair or replacement.

1 Reach Out

Message us on WhatsApp or visit our shop at St Michaels GSM Center, Aba. Tell us the issue and share your purchase details.

2 Free Diagnostics

We'll inspect the device at no cost to confirm the fault is covered under warranty. This usually takes 24–48 hours.

3 Repair or Replace

Depending on the fault, we'll repair the device with genuine parts or replace it entirely — whichever is faster for you.

4 Pickup or Delivery

Collect your repaired device from our shop, or we can deliver it back to you anywhere in Nigeria at no extra charge.

What's Not Covered

Physical damage from drops, water damage, cracked screens from accidents, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear-and-tear (like scratches) are not covered. If you're unsure, just ask — we'll always be honest with you.
